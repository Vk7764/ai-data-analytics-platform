# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 November 2026**


This Privacy Policy explains what data AI Data Analytics Platform ("the Service") collects, how it's used, and your choices.

## 1. What We Collect

**Account information**
- Email address
- Optional profile details you choose to add (name, organization)
- Password (stored as a salted, hashed value — we never store or see your actual password)

**Uploaded data**
- Files you upload (CSV, Excel, JSON, SQL) and the data they contain.

**Usage data**
- Basic technical logs (e.g. IP address, timestamps) for security, rate-limiting, and debugging.

**Chat and analysis history**
- Questions you ask and the results generated, stored so you can revisit past conversations.

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- To provide the Service's features (cleaning, charts, insights, forecasting, chat, reports).
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
- To send you account-related emails (e.g. email verification and password reset links).
- To improve and maintain the Service.

We do not sell your data.

## 3. Sharing with Third-Party AI Providers
To power AI-driven features, portions of your data (such as column names, sample values, and the questions you type) are sent to one or more of the third-party AI providers listed below, depending on availability, or to a locally-run model (Ollama) that does not leave our server. We do not control how these third parties handle data beyond what their own policies state — please review them if you have concerns:
- **OpenRouter:** OpenRouter does not use Inputs or Outputs for model training. However, the downstream model providers it routes requests to may retain or use Inputs and Outputs for training or improvement, unless a no-training provider is selected or an enterprise Data Processing Agreement applies. Policy: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
- **Anthropic:** Anthropic may use Inputs and Outputs to train or improve its models unless you opt out, and content flagged for safety or reported by users may still be used. Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- **Google (Gemini):** Google's consumer Privacy Policy is broad. Gemini, API and Cloud contexts may be governed by service-specific notices or a Data Processing Agreement, so the consumer policy alone should not be assumed to describe how API customer data is handled. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Groq:** Groq's consumer Privacy Policy does not apply to Customer Data that Groq processes as a data processor for GroqCloud and API Cloud Services. The Groq Services Agreement and Data Processing Addendum govern that data. Policy: https://groq.com/privacy-policy
- **Hugging Face:** Hugging Face may process and store data in the United States and other countries and uses subprocessors. Retention and deletion requests can be sent to privacy@huggingface.co. Policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy
- **Mistral AI:** Policy: https://mistral.ai/privacy-policy

**How this Service handles AI outputs:** AI-generated outputs are cached for up to 14 days so the same request is not repeated. They are deleted when the related dataset is deleted. You can request deletion of your account or any dataset at any time (see Section 4). We do not sell personal data.

**We recommend not uploading highly sensitive data** (government ID numbers, financial account credentials, health records, etc.) given this sharing.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
- Your account and uploaded datasets are stored on our servers until you delete them.
- AI-generated explanations are cached for up to 14 days so the same request isn't repeated, and they are deleted when you delete the dataset.
- You can delete any dataset at any time from the app; this permanently removes it from our storage.
- You can permanently delete your account and all its data at any time from the Account section of the app (we ask you to confirm by email and password), or by contacting us (see Section 8).

## 5. Security
We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data (encrypted password storage, access controls, rate limiting). No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
